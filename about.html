About RRYVS

RRYVS is a Kanpur-based company operating in FMCG manufacturing and international trade. We focus on doing the basics right: high product quality, fair pricing, and relationships built to last.

Our drinking water plant is equipped with modern purification systems including RO, UV, and ozone treatment to ensure safe and consistent water quality. We support multiple bottle sizes and offer bulk supply as well as private labeling solutions for commercial clients.

Alongside water manufacturing, we export a wide range of whole and powdered spices such as chilli, turmeric, cumin, and coriander. Our spices are sourced from reliable suppliers, processed under hygienic conditions, and packed to meet domestic and international export standards.

We believe strong processes create strong partnerships. From sourcing to packaging, every step of our operation is designed to deliver consistency, safety, and value to our customers.
