Water Plant & Technology

Our water plant is planned and commissioned with precision. Every element, from concept design to final execution, is carefully structured. This includes product and packaging development, plant layout, machine utilities, drainage systems, and electrical planning.

The facility supports a wide range of beverage formats, both carbonated and non-carbonated, alcoholic and non-alcoholic. Production capabilities include packaged and mineral water, alkaline and added-mineral water, flavoured and sparkling water, pulp-based and synthetic juices, still wine, sparkling wine, and carbonated wine.

This setup allows operational efficiency, strict quality control, and flexibility to expand across beverage categories.
